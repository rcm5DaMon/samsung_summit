Source: BigQuery e-smart-433113 · sales and stock: region_data.sales_vs_stock (completed days to , stock ) · today: region_data.sales_daily (PAID POS) · targets: master_data.target_summit_november, master_data.target_aseries_samsung and master_data.target_racing_samsung · store attributes: sales_b2c.master_store_r5.

Summit Device = SAMSUNG, Product_Category = Device units excluding Tablet (Detail_Cat = TABLET is left out). A Series = SAMSUNG Device with Detail_Cat = A SERIES. Racing = SAMSUNG Device with Detail_Cat = S SERIES or Z SERIES, 5 tier targets per store reached in order (tier 1 first). Stores = every store in the target files plus every Region 5 site that sold Samsung Device this month, last month or today in business units 1MULTIBRAND, 5JB, 6OS, 3NASA or an exhibition (never 7ERABLUE); stores without a target in a view still add their sales to every total and show "no target". Racing tier is set by the month-end estimate (SUM_month_est_quantity, S+Z): under T1 = "Under T1"; passes T1 but below T2 = "Tier 2"; passes T2 but below T3 = "Tier 3"; passes T3 but below T4 = "Tier 4"; passes T4 but below T5 = "Tier 5"; passes T5 = "Above T5". Racing growth MoM index = estimate ÷ last month S+Z qty × 100; a store is growing when the index is above 100. Group rows (region, BU, LOB, TSH) use summed tier targets, estimates and last-month qty. MTD = SUM_quantity for completed days. Estimate = SUM_month_est_quantity (completed-day run-rate × 30). On track (MTD) = MTD ≥ target × days gone ÷ 30. On track (estimate) = estimate ≥ target. Today = PAID POS orders in sales_daily, only counted when its date is after the last completed day. Gap = target − (MTD + today). Need / day = gap ÷ days left incl. today. Cover (days) = (stock ÷ month-end estimate qty) × 30.42.
